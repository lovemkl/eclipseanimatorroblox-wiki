## What Is Eclipse Animator?

A Roblox Studio animation plugin released in October 2026, inspired by Blender's animation workflow and positioned as the flagship free alternative to Moon Animator. Its feature set: inverse kinematics with a per-limb toggle, unparenting for complex rigs, live editing while the timeline plays, and support for any Roblox animation type. The complete guide covers the full story; the features pages cover each capability in depth.

## Wasn't It Free? What Happened?

It launched with a limited free-claim window — roughly three days — during which claiming the plugin from the Roblox Creator Store tied it permanently to your account at zero cost. When the window closed, the listing switched to paid-only. Claimed copies are unaffected: they remain in their owners' inventories permanently, updates included. The claim guide documents the mechanics and the aftermath in full — including what the switch means for people deciding whether to buy now.

## I Missed the Window. Should I Buy It?

It depends entirely on your animation volume, and the comparisons exist precisely for this decision. Working animators — Studio daily or weekly, cutscenes and character work — amortize the price almost immediately against the workflow speed the IK toggle and live editing deliver. Occasional animators with single-rig, simple needs are honestly served by free options: Studio's built-in Animation Editor and the open-source Moon Animator 2 build on GitHub both cover fundamentals at no cost. The built-in comparison page maps exactly where "free and adequate" ends and the paid capability begins.

## How Is It Different from Moon Animator?

Workflow philosophy, mostly. Moon Animator is the established standard: Studio-native conventions, years of maturity, an ocean of tutorials, multi-rig strength — and no IK, no live editing, no unparenting. Eclipse Animator imports the Blender interaction model: viewport-direct posing, the IK toggle, live editing, unparenting — the features Moon Animator lacks as headline items, in a tool that is weeks old rather than years. Animators from external 3D suites tend to convert instantly; Moon Animator lifetimes report an adjustment curve. The Moon Animator comparison runs the full table and does conclude.

## What Can It Actually Animate?

Standard character rigs completely (walk cycles, combat, emotes — the core), multi-rig scenes and cutscenes on one timeline (the historical Moon Animator strength, carried forward), and tween-style part motion for authored sequences. Custom rigs and exotic setups work through the unparenting system with growing community knowledge. The animation types page carries the honest map, including the edge cases — the claim "any Roblox animation type" means what the platform's rigs and parts can express, which is a large territory with named borders.

## Will the Free Window Come Back?

The precedent exists (this launch), the incentive is arguable (updates and version milestones are natural occasions), and the policy is theirs.
