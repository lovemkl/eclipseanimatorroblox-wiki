## Who Made It, and Why That Matters

Eclipse Animator is credited to riftkuro and seven, independent developers working inside the Roblox creator ecosystem rather than a studio or a platform team. That origin shows in the design choices. The plugin does not read like a committee product — it reads like two people who animate, who knew exactly which frictions hurt, and who shipped a tool shaped like the fixes. The Blender-inspired interaction model, the per-limb IK switch rather than a global mode, the decision to launch free-to-claim: these are opinionated moves, and opinionated moves come from small teams with taste.

The community context made the landing softer. For years the DevForum's "Moon Animator alternatives" thread accumulated replies from animators priced out of the standard tool; free options existed but none carried the flagship flag. When Eclipse Animator arrived carrying it — with launch coverage, a warning-titled DevForum announcement urging everyone to claim before the paid switch, and short-form videos pulling hundreds of thousands of views — the demand that had pooled for years moved in a single week. A thirty-two-second teaser alone crossed well past four hundred thousand views, and the official tutorial passed two hundred thousand within days. Plugins do not usually become community events. This one did.

## The Feature Set, Plainly

What Eclipse Animator actually gives you, stripped of launch-week adrenaline:

**IK/FK switching.** Pose limbs joint-by-joint (forward kinematics) or drag the endpoint and let the solver arrange the chain (inverse kinematics) — and switch between the two per limb, mid-animation. This is the plugin's headline capability, and the feature that most changes day-one posing speed for animators arriving from other tools. The rigging page covers the system in full.

**Blender rig import.** The bridge feature that made the community lean forward: rigs authored in Blender can be brought into the Eclipse Animator workflow, which means the enormous Blender animation ecosystem — its tutorials, its asset conventions, its animators — has an on-ramp into Roblox Studio through this plugin. For studios that prototype in Blender and ship in Roblox, the import path collapses a translation step that used to be manual.

**Unparenting and live editing.** Animate parts independently of hierarchy chains for prop and interaction work, and refine keys while the timeline plays rather than scrub-edit-scrub. These two features carry multi-rig cutscene work, which the animation types page maps in detail.

**Any Roblox animation type.** The plugin drives character rigs, multi-rig scenes and part motion on one timeline — the breadth claim the launch made and the coverage on this wiki verifies as delivered.

## The Claim Window, Explained Once More

Because it remains the most-searched fact about Eclipse Animator: the plugin launched free-to-claim for a limited window of roughly three days in mid-October 2026. Claiming from the Roblox Creator Store during the window tied the plugin to your Roblox account permanently — not a device, not a subscription, the account — and claimed copies receive updates like any holder's. When the window closed, the listing switched to paid-only. Free owners noticed nothing; everyone else began the buying decision.
